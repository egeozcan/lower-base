FIELD MANUAL

HOW TO PLAY

Command the blue army from the southern base. Destroy every red unit and structure to win. You lose when your entire force and base are destroyed.

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard on the main menu. Easy sends slow, small patrols. Medium uses adaptive counters and regular waves. Hard adapts between pressure, expansion, counters, defense, and finishing attacks. Its plans and assault timing vary each match. Both sides have equal starting credits, income, costs, build times, and unit stats in every mode. The AI sees the open battlefield, but cannot read your production queues or sheltered units. Demo uses the selected difficulty for both commanders.

01 / BUILD YOUR FORCE

Use the production buttons to queue units. Rifle infantry and rocket troopers train at the barracks; tanks, demolition trucks, and ore collectors build at the factory. Credits replenish over time. Each building has 9 shared slots for queued and garrisoned units, shown in a 3×3 icon grid above it. Garrisoned units have a blue background and a check mark. Click one to eject it and free its slot: troops gather during Retreat, while collectors start mining. Click a queued unit to cancel and refund its cost (up to the credit cap). Canceling the active unit resets its progress. Keep your production buildings alive.

At equal spending, rifles strongly counter rocket troopers, rockets strongly counter tanks, and tanks strongly counter rifles. Mix your forces to protect each unit’s weakness. Hard AI may send a demolition truck to intercept an unopposed wave, so keep ranged counters ready.

02 / DEPLOYMENT MODES

Gather holds new troops in front of your base. Click a gathered unit to select it; Shift-click to add or remove units. On a touchscreen, tap units to toggle selection. Select Send to commit those troops.

Attack commits all gathered troops and sends new troops automatically. Use Retreat to recall deployed troops to your base rally area. All newly completed units stay inside their production buildings until you select Gather or Attack. Troops inside a destroyed building are lost. Use Base to zoom in for selection and Map to see the battlefield.

03 / KEEP THE CREDITS FLOWING

Ore collectors cost 200 credits and automatically mine ore and deliver it to your refinery. Deployed collectors are unarmed and keep mining during Retreat. Collectors completed during Retreat stay inside the factory until Gather or Attack releases them, then begin mining. Protect your collectors and refinery; ore fields eventually run dry.

Demolition trucks cost 250 credits and explode on attack or death, damaging allies as well as enemies. Keep them away from your army. Only one may be alive or queued per side.

04 / WATCH OR TAKE A BREAK

Select Demo on the main menu to watch two computer commanders manage their own production and attack waves. Production and troop commands are disabled while spectating; you can still switch views.

Pause freezes the match, including production and income. Resume continues it. Abort ends the current game or demo and returns to the main menu. Starting again creates a fresh battlefield.

05 / PREPARE YOUR DEFENSE

Each base has two fixed anti-tank pads, marked by rings in front of the buildings. Use Left Pad / Right Pad or 8 / 9 to build a gun for 200 credits. Construction takes 8 seconds and runs independently of your unit queues. The gun can be destroyed while building and cannot fire until ready.

Guns have 320 HP, outrange tanks, and prioritize armor. Their slow traverse and weak anti-infantry damage leave them vulnerable to rifles, which deal double their usual building damage to guns. Protect your defenses with troops. Rebuild a destroyed gun for full cost while your command center survives. Empty pads do not count toward victory. Medium and Hard AI can build the same defenses.
