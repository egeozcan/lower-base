FIELD MANUAL

HOW TO PLAY

Command the blue army from the southern base. Destroy every red unit and structure to win. You lose when your entire force and base are destroyed.

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard on the main menu. Easy sends slow, small patrols. Medium uses adaptive counters and regular waves. Hard plans army matchups, expands its economy when safe, and coordinates assaults. Both sides have equal starting credits, income, costs, build times, and unit stats in every mode. The AI sees the open battlefield, but cannot read your production queues or sheltered units. Demo uses the selected difficulty for both commanders.

01 / BUILD YOUR FORCE

Use the production buttons to queue units. Rifle infantry and rocket troopers train at the barracks; tanks, demolition trucks, and ore collectors build at the factory. Credits replenish over time. Keep your production buildings alive.

Rifles counter rockets, rockets counter armor, and tanks counter infantry. Mix your forces to answer the enemy army.

02 / DEPLOYMENT MODES

Gather holds new troops in front of your base. Click a gathered unit to select it; Shift-click to add or remove units. On a touchscreen, tap units to toggle selection. Select Send to commit those troops.

Attack commits all gathered troops and sends new troops automatically. Use Retreat to recall deployed troops to your base rally area. All newly completed units stay inside their production buildings until you select Gather or Attack. Troops inside a destroyed building are lost. Use Base to zoom in for selection and Map to see the battlefield.

03 / KEEP THE CREDITS FLOWING

Ore collectors cost 200 credits and automatically mine ore and deliver it to your refinery. Deployed collectors are unarmed and keep mining during Retreat. Collectors completed during Retreat stay inside the factory until Gather or Attack releases them, then begin mining. Protect your collectors and refinery; ore fields eventually run dry.

Demolition trucks cost 250 credits and explode on attack or death, damaging allies as well as enemies. Keep them away from your army. Only one may be alive or queued per side.

04 / WATCH OR TAKE A BREAK

Select Demo on the main menu to watch two computer commanders manage their own production and attack waves. Production and troop commands are disabled while spectating; you can still switch views.

Pause freezes the match, including production and income. Resume continues it. Abort ends the current game or demo and returns to the main menu. Starting again creates a fresh battlefield.
